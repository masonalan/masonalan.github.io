Granular Synth

a collaboration with Ritual Object

We are building a granular synth with an Arduino and designing a case for it. Granular synthesis is the process of taking a small sample, or "grain", of some audio (e.g. 0.1s), looping it quickly to create a new sound.

tyWorld

tyWorld is a simple OpenGL planet simulator written in C++. The planets are cubes and the asteroid belts are rendered using instanced rendering. The focus of this project was to learn about different types of lighting.
